# Veylora RIP

**Veylora RIP** is a native, high-end direct-to-film RIP focused initially on one converted Epson ET-8550 configuration.

The project is personal-use first, with architecture suitable for future distribution. Its defining promise is independent control over process and white channels without requiring Digital Factory, AcroRIP, PrintFab, or another commercial RIP at runtime.

## Working positioning

> **Veylora RIP**  
> Native control for every printed layer.

## Product family candidates

- **Veylora RIP:** native rendering, separation, calibration, screening, and printer output.
- **Veylora Studio:** future artwork preparation and gang-sheet layout layer.
- **Veylora Lab:** future calibration, profiling, diagnostics, and test-target tools.
- **Veylora Devices:** future printer backend packages.

These are naming directions, not a commitment to build multiple products now.
