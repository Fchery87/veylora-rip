## What the workflows imply for this RIP

- **Confirm the physical ink map with a print.** Store logical CMYK and white separately from physical slots and require a labeled slot test when setting up a conversion. A preview can verify the separation but cannot identify which ink was actually poured into a slot. The same model can use different ink assignments depending on the conversion and driver. [Fiery ET-8550 V2 notes](https://help.fiery.com/FieryDigitalFactory/ReadMe/Epson_ET8550_v2_en_US.pdf), [PrintFab mappings](https://www.zedonet.com/en_support_dtf.phtml), [AcroRIP changes](https://acrorip.com/updates.php)
- **Give white its own preview and controls.** Start from image alpha and expose white coverage and edge adjustments. Preserve intentional white artwork when a source pixel is opaque, and keep fully transparent areas clear by default. Treat white under dark colors, white highlights, and any explicit spot-white layer as separate controls with defined precedence. Show the design on a garment color, the film orientation, and the white plate in isolation. On the film, the white backing is printed over the color so that it sits behind the color after transfer. [Caldera white settings](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/15207936811921), [Caldera layer preview](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/13766611032849), [PrintFab DTF setup](https://www.zedonet.com/en_support_dtf.phtml), [SAi garment preview](https://support.thinksai.com/hc/en-us/articles/43516581716244-DTF-PRO-New-Features)
- **Separate artwork knockout, white choke, and device screening.** Knockout lets the garment supply a selected artwork color. A fully knocked-out pixel should emit zero coverage in both color and white, including after applying a spread or minimum-white setting. Test with the chosen garment because small amounts of color ink can pick up adhesive and appear as a pale haze. White choke changes the backing edge, while artwork halftoning creates openings in the transfer for the garment to show through. Device screening encodes ink coverage as printable dots. [Fiery DTF tuning](https://communities.fiery.com/s/article/Optimising-DTF-Output), [Caldera color removal](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/28987096899729), [Caldera white settings](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/15207936811921), [SAi artwork halftones](https://support.thinksai.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/43516583724564), [Fiery device screening](https://help.fiery.com/cws/FieryXF/7.3_cws_6.8/en-us/GUID-EB236779-433C-4A4F-A574-ECA1F04347A3.html)
- **Bind color to a measured print condition.** Record the ink map, ink and film, resolution, screening, ink limits, and white settings alongside the output ICC profile. Commercial tools distinguish per-channel response calibration and combined ink limits from ICC profile creation. For this project, measure validation patches after transfer to a known garment under a recorded press process, because the customer sees the pressed result. Importing an ICC profile can be a separate milestone from building a measurement wizard. [Caldera calibration workflow](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/15789364258321), [ICC profile creation](https://www.color.org/creatingprofiles/), [Epson transfer workflow](https://files.support.epson.com/docid/other/cmp0437-00_en.pdf)
- **Make sheet production repeatable before automating it.** Save exact artwork sizes, copies, rotations, spacing, placement, and the print preset with a gang sheet. Provide a printable-area preview and mirror the sheet once for transfer. Fiery's saved layouts and queue presets, and Caldera's success/error routing, show how these repeatable settings support later hot-folder ingestion, auto-nesting, job labels, and barcodes. [Fiery gang-sheet builder](https://www.fiery.com/products/digital-factory-dtf/), [Caldera destination settings](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/13766611032849), [Caldera hot folders](https://helpdesk.caldera.com/hc/en-us/articles/39817023150481)
